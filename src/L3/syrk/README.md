# syrk — symmetric and Hermitian rank-k update

$$C \leftarrow \alpha\,\mathrm{op}(A)\,\mathrm{op}(A)^{T} + \beta C,$$

one triangle of the $n \times n$ symmetric $C$ stored and updated,
the other never written. Six routines: `dsyrk`, `ssyrk` (real),
`zherk`, `cherk` (Hermitian: $A A^{H}$, $\alpha$ and $\beta$ real, the
stored diagonal ends exactly real), `zsyrk`, `csyrk` (complex
symmetric: $A A^{T}$, $\alpha$ and $\beta$ complex, no diagonal
fix-up). $\mathrm{op}(A)$ is $n \times k$.

```text
dsyrk(uplo, trans, n, k, alpha, a, lda, beta, c, ldc, work)
```

`work` is at least `dsyrk_work(trans, n, k)` elements: the packed
panels for the no-transpose form, plus an $n \times k$ transposed copy
for the `Trans` form, which runs the no-transpose kernel on the copy
(bit-identical to the definition by construction).

## What the result is, exactly

For each stored element, $\beta c_{ij}$ then the products
$a_{il}\,(\alpha a_{jl})$ added in ascending $l$: the plain column
sweep's order, which the packed shape reproduces bit for bit (tested
against the exported sweep on every tail and block edge, the unstored
triangle NaN-poisoned and checked untouched). The Hermitian forms set
the diagonal's imaginary part to exactly zero after each column, the
reference's `DBLE()` convention; with $\beta = 1$ and nothing to add
they return before touching $C$, as the reference does.

## How it is computed

**f64 and f32: the gemm tile over the stored triangle** (`*syrk_packed`,
the K1 campaign). $A$'s rows are packed as gemm's left panel, and the
same rows are packed again as the right panel, read as
$\mathrm{op}(B) = A^{T}$ with $\alpha$ folded in, so the operation is
gemm's broadcast micro-kernel over its own operand. Tiles entirely in
the unstored triangle are skipped; tiles that cross the diagonal are
computed into a scratch tile, which carries the partial sums across
the $k$-blocks, and copied back only where stored. The $M_R$-row and
four-column tails keep the plain sweep.

**The complex forms: the truncated four-column sweep** (`CA+FO` in
`DESIGN.md`'s taxonomy). Four stored columns are grouped so each
column of $A$ streams once per group over the common triangle segment,
the ragged edge handled scalar in the same order. The Hermitian
scalar for column $j$ is $\alpha\,\overline{a_{jl}}$, the complex
symmetric one $\alpha\,a_{jl}$.

**Exported shapes.** `dsyrk_sweep`/`ssyrk_sweep`, the fan-out sweep
that shipped before K1 and is the tests' bit-identity reference;
`dsyrk_packed`/`ssyrk_packed` with their `*_packed_work` queries.

## Performance

**The K1 race** (`bench/k1-ab.mjs`): the packed shape against the
exported sweep, both called directly with the workspace outside the
timed call, folds asserted identical. Figures are `sweep ÷ packed`
wall time, upper / lower triangle, the range across runner runs
40231405123 and 40232089887:

| shape (n×k) | f64 upper | f64 lower | f32 upper | f32 lower |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 256² | 2.06–2.09 | 1.93–1.97 | 2.10–2.13 | 2.33–2.41 |
| 512² | 2.05–2.07 | 1.88–1.90 | 2.04 | 2.43–2.47 |
| 1024² | 2.37–2.43 | 2.02–2.04 | 1.99–2.00 | 2.58–2.62 |
| 512×2048 | 2.05–2.06 | 1.87–1.90 | 2.06 | 2.47–2.49 |
| 2048×128 | 2.32 | 1.96–1.97 | 1.99 | 2.59–2.63 |

**Against OpenBLAS and BLIS in the same engine** (the Level 3 README
has the table): dsyrk at n = 512 ties OpenBLAS-wasm (0.99–1.05× of its
time, 9.2–9.9 ms against 9.2–9.4) and takes half of BLIS's.

**Against the machine** (n = 512, same-run peak probe, two draws; the
probe's defect is in the Level 3 README): syrk f64 98–102%, f32
99–110%; herk c64 72–95%, c32 76–94%. The real rows run at the gemm
kernel's rate; the complex rows are the sweep's.

## Tests

`tests/L3/dsyrk.rs`, `ssyrk.rs`, `zherk.rs`, `cherk.rs`, `zsyrk.rs`,
`csyrk.rs`: the bit-for-bit replay of the sweep's order; the packed
shape bit-identical to the exported sweep for n = 0..23 and 31, 64,
65, 130, 257 with k across the $K_C$ edge, both triangles, three
$(\alpha, \beta)$ pairs; the `Trans` form equal to the no-transpose
call on an explicit transpose; the Hermitian diagonal exactly real;
`semantics.rs` for the $\alpha = 0$, $k = 0$ and $\beta = 1$ rules;
`errors.rs` for the argument checks.
